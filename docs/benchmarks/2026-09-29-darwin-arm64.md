### Throughput (no per-event timing)

The three independent process-level throughput medians were 7.37, 7.99, and
7.83 M events/s. The median of those three measurements is reported below rather
than selecting the fastest run.

| Median of process medians | Mean time per event |
|---:|---:|
| **7.83 M events/s** | **127.8 ns** |

Individual runs showed sensitivity to background scheduling: the within-process
min/max spread was 3.09%, 3.15%, and 16.69%, respectively.
